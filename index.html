How we built it

Where the numbers come from

1 / The data

Every animal-related crash reported to police in Iowa in 2025, from the Iowa Department of Transportation's public crash records. We did not estimate or invent any of it.

2 / Grouping

We grouped crashes that happened within about 4 km of each other into one corridor, then ranked corridors by how many crashes they contain.

3 / What changed

Our first version shaded the map by where deer live. A wildlife biologist at the Iowa DNR told us crash risk follows traffic volume, not deer density. So we rebuilt it around crash counts instead.

4 / What it can't do

It shows a pattern from the past. It cannot tell you an animal is there right now, and it only contains crashes that got reported — which is roughly 6 out of every 10.
